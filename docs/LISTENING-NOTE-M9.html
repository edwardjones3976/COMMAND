M9 listening note

The tests measure the alarm line's loudness and its peaks. They cannot say whether it is loud enough to get you up. That is your sign-off, and it has not been given.

What to do

  1. Install the debug build of this branch on a phone with an offline speech voice, or on your emulator.
  2. Put the phone's alarm volume at maximum.
  3. Make Reveille Max the active profile (alert intensity 5) and use "Test this alarm now". Listen to the spoken line over the tone, on the phone's own speaker.
  4. Repeat with a profile at alert intensity 1 to 3, such as Steady.

What to listen for

  • Is the spoken line clearly louder than before this change, and loud enough at intensity 5?
  • Is it still clean? Listen for crackle, pumping, or breaths and hiss brought up between words.
  • Is intensity 5 audibly louder than intensity 1 to 3? The difference is only 2 LU by design, so it may be subtle.

What the targets are

-16 LUFS at intensity 1 to 3, -15 at 4, -14 at 5, peaks no higher than -1 dBTP. If it is too quiet, say so: the targets are one small table and can be raised, though dense speech runs into the ceiling at about -11 LUFS.

What you will not hear yet

The per-voice loudness trim has no effect in the app, because no voice packs are stored yet. Studio (Gemini) voices arrive in M14.
